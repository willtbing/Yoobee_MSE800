There are five entities in this ER-diagram: Student, Enrollment, Lecture, Lecturer, and Subjects.

Write the type of relationship between the entities and describe it.
The relationship between Student and Enrollment is one-to-many. A student can sign up for multiple courses such as CS, Math, Physics, etc., thus there will be multiple Enrollment records. And an erollment belongs to only one student. 
The relationship between Enrollment and Lecture is many-to-one. TAn Enrollment corresponds to a specific Lecture. A Lecture can have 0 or more Enrollments because a class can have many students participating.
The relationship between Student and Lecture is many-to-many. A student can enrol in many lectures. A lecture can have many students.
The relationship between Lecturer and Lecture is one-to-many. One lecture should have one lecturer, and each lecturer should have more than one lecture.
The relationship between Lecture and Subjects is many-to-one. A Subject can contain multiple Lectures. A Lecture belongs to a Subject.
The relationship between Lecturer and Subjects is many-to-many. A lecturer could attend more than one subjects, and a subject could have more than one lecturers.
