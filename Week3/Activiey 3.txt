The relationship between Student and Enrollment is more-to-one. Each student can only enroll once, and the enrollment could include more than one student.
The relationship between Student and Lecture is more-to-one. To enroll, each student could attend a lucture, and the lecture could include more than one student.
The relationship between Enrollment and Lecture is one-to-one. There should a lecture for enrollment.
The relationship between Lecturer and Lecture is one-to-more. One lecture should have one lecturer, and each lecturer should have more than one lecture.
The relationship between Lecture and Subjects is more-to-more. A lecture could contain more than one subjects, and a subject could have more than one lectures.
The relationship between Lecturer and Subjects is more-to-more. A lecturer could attend more than one subjects, and a subject could have more than one lecturers.
